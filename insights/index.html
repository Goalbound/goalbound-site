Massapequa or Islip? A town's record is mostly luck.

One Long Island town's D1 players start; another's sit. Shuffle every player's hometown at random and towns like both appear anyway. A town needs about 100 players before its record is half signal.

24 Sep 2026

Canada is the most under-recruited source in men's college lacrosse.

Per roster spot, Ontario produces more All-Americans than any US state, and Canadian players start more and sit less. They fill one Division I roster spot in twenty.
